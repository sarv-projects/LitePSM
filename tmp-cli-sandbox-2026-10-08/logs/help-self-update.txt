LITESPM SELF-UPDATE

Check for a newer LiteSPM binary and replace this one atomically.

USAGE
  litespm self-update [--force] [--help]

WHAT IT DOES
  - Fetches the published release manifest, and when a newer version exists
     downloads the release ASSET (never the release page), verifies its
     SHA-256 checksum, and replaces the running executable atomically.
  - `--force` reinstalls or downgrades even when the manifest version is not
     newer.

WHAT IT DOES NOT
  - Does not update installed packages or skills — that is `litespm skills
     update`.
  - Refuses to fall back to an unverified download when signature verification
     is required and unavailable.

EXAMPLES
  1. litespm self-update
  2. litespm self-update --force

EXIT CODES
  0  success, including nothing to do
  1  refused or failed
  2  usage error (unknown flag, missing argument, unknown command)
