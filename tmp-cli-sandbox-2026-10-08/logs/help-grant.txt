LITESPM GRANT

Issue, list and revoke the grants that authorize tool invocations.

USAGE
  litespm grant <capability-id>
  litespm grant list
  litespm grant revoke <capability-id>

WHAT IT DOES
  - `grant <capability-id>` shows what the tool may do and records your
     decision, bound to the capability's current input schema; `list` shows
     recorded grants and `revoke` withdraws one.
  - Grants are what the policy engine checks before an invocation runs through
     the bridge.

WHAT IT DOES NOT
  - Refuses to run without an interactive terminal: a grant is given by a
     person, not scripted by the process asking for it.
  - Does not grant an undiscovered capability (`litespm capabilities refresh`
     first), and a grant stops matching once the tool's input schema changes
     — you re-approve it.

EXAMPLES
  1. litespm grant <capability-id>
  2. litespm grant list
  3. litespm grant revoke <capability-id>

EXIT CODES
  0  success, including nothing to do
  1  refused or failed
  2  usage error (unknown flag, missing argument, unknown command)
